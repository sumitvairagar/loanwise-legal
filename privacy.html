LoanWise — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 June 2026

LoanWise ("the App") is built and maintained by Sumit Vairagar ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the App collects, how we use it, and your rights. We keep it short on purpose — the App is designed to keep your financial information on your device.

Summary in plain English

  • Your loan details, payments, and simulations stay on your device. We never see them.
  • We use RevenueCat to process in-app subscriptions. RevenueCat sees your purchase activity and an anonymous user identifier — nothing about your loans.
  • We do not run analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share your data with anyone.

Data the App handles on your device

All loan data — loan amounts, interest rates, EMI history, payment records, simulator scenarios, app settings — is stored locally using on-device storage (Hive). It never leaves your device, syncs to a cloud, or reaches our servers. If you delete the App, this data is removed with it.

Data RevenueCat collects (for in-app subscriptions)

If you choose to subscribe to LoanWise Pro, our subscription provider RevenueCat collects:

  • Purchase history — which plan you bought, when, renewal status. Used to confirm your entitlement.
  • Anonymous user identifier — a random ID generated on first launch. Used only to associate your subscription with your device across reinstalls or restores.

RevenueCat does not receive your name, email, location, or any loan details. RevenueCat's own privacy policy applies to the data they handle: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

Apple's role

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. Apple's standard subscription handling and receipt validation apply. We never see your payment method.

What we do not do

  • We do not run analytics or behavior-tracking SDKs.
  • We do not use advertising IDs.
  • We do not share, sell, rent, or trade any data.
  • We do not contact you unless you email us first.

Your rights

  • Delete your data: Uninstall the App to delete all locally stored data. Cancel your Pro subscription through Apple's subscription settings.
  • Restore purchases: Use the "Restore purchase" button on the paywall to re-link a Pro entitlement to a new install.
  • Questions or concerns: Email us at the address below.

Children's privacy

LoanWise is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the updated text replaces this page and the "Last updated" date changes. Material changes will be noted in the App's release notes.
