What LoanWise does

LoanWise is a loan simulator and tracker for iPhone. The simulator lets you model any loan — home, car, personal — and instantly see the impact of prepayments, EMI bumps, or rate cuts. If you decide to save a loan, LoanWise becomes a private, on-device tracker for your real payments and progress.

Common questions

Is my data private?

Yes. Every loan, payment, and simulation stays on your device. We never see your financial data. Full details in our Privacy Policy.

What's free vs Pro?

The full simulator — all three scenarios (Prepay, Bump EMI, Rate cut) and the impact calculations — is free. LoanWise Pro adds the ability to save loans, log real payments, track loans over time, and run scenarios on your saved loans.

How do I restore my Pro subscription on a new phone?

Open Settings → Upgrade to Pro → tap Restore purchase. As long as you're signed in to the same Apple ID that bought Pro, it'll reactivate.

Where are my loans backed up?

Settings → Data → Export Backup writes a JSON file you can save to iCloud Drive, email to yourself, or store anywhere. Use Import Backup to restore on a new device.

I lost a simulation — can I get it back?

Simulator data clears when you fully quit the App. To keep a loan, tap Save to my loans at the bottom of the Simulator (Pro feature).

A payment shows the wrong amount.

Open the loan, find the payment row, swipe or tap to edit. Edits update the schedule immediately.

The App crashed.

Email us with what you were doing and your iPhone model. We respond within 48 hours.

Subscriptions

LoanWise Pro is a subscription billed by Apple. To manage or cancel:

iPhone → Settings → tap your name at the top → Subscriptions → LoanWise

Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep Pro access until the end of the current period.
